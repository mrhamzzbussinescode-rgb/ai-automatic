## Publishing and review rules

1. These rows are ideas, not upload authorization. Use original or properly licensed visuals and audio; don't imply generated scenes are real footage.
2. Prepare and review the actual video, title, description, visibility, and publish time before scheduling. Get approval for the exact post.
3. Keep a traceable record of the draft, approval, scheduled time, provider result, and any retry. Review real engaged views and watch time weekly; do not infer success from views alone.
4. This calendar does not schedule uploads by itself. A finished video asset is needed for the first post.
